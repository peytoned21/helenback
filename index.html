    Read before you leave Atlanta

    Three things that will wreck the weekend if you skip them.
    Dry county

    Buy the booze on the way

    Union County doesn't allow package sales. Restaurants can pour, but nobody in the county can sell you beer, wine or liquor to take back to the cabin. Licensed wineries are the one exception.

    Stock up in Helen (Discount Spirits of Helen) or Hiawassee (Hiawassee Package Store). Both are closed Sundays.

    Directions

    Do not turn on Cindy Cove

    Google will try to route you down Cindy Cove Rd: a one-lane dirt road with potholes, rocks and downed trees. Turn on Old Bald Mountain Rd off Gainesville Hwy 19 instead. Apple Maps gets it right.

    Want to skip the switchbacks over Blood Mountain? Take 575/515 through Ellijay and Blue Ridge. The last 0.8 mi is packed gravel, ending at a red covered bridge.

    Timing

    Check-in is 4:00 PM

    Early check-in at noon costs $200 and only happens if the owners text Peyton 24 hours ahead. No text, no early check-in.

    Park around back by the wheelchair ramp. That door is the easiest one for unloading. Door and gate codes are in the group text.

    03

    Hikes

    The cabin sits at the foot of Blood Mountain, so the trails are close.

    Saturday morning · everyone

    The pick · 10 min away

    Bear Hair Gap Loop

    4.1 mi loop~935 ft gainModerate

    Starts at Vogel State Park's backcountry trailhead and climbs to an overlook of Lake Trahlyta and the mountains behind it. Back in time for lunch. State park parking fee.

    5 more hikes
    The big one · ~20 min

    Blood Mountain

    4.3 mi out & back~1,450 ft gainStrenuous

    Byron Reece trailhead at Neels Gap, then the Appalachian Trail to its highest summit in Georgia and an old stone shelter on top. The lot fills early on Saturdays, so leave by 8.

    Best view · ~30 min

    Brasstown Bald

    1.2 mi paved~400 ft gainShort & steep

    Georgia's highest point. The deck looks over four states. Opens at 10 AM, $8 a person (16 and up), and a shuttle can run anyone who'd rather skip the climb.

    Friday · while the guys golf

    Easiest · 10–15 min

    Lake Trahlyta + Helton Creek Falls

    1.6 mi totalFlatEasy

    A 1-mile loop around the lake at Vogel with a spur to Trahlyta Falls, then a short drive to Helton Creek's two waterfalls, about 0.6 miles round trip. The last stretch to Helton Creek is a gravel forest road.

    Two waterfalls · ~25 min

    DeSoto Falls

    2 mi out & back~160 ft gainEasy

    Over a footbridge and along Frogtown Creek to the lower falls, then the big upper cascade at about a mile. Small self-pay day-use fee, so bring cash.

    Big payoff · ~40 min

    Preachers Rock

    2 mi out & back~500 ft gainEasy, steep finish

    Appalachian Trail from Woody Gap to one of the best views in north Georgia. The last quarter mile is switchbacks. Pair it with lunch in Dahlonega on the way back.

    Drive times are rough estimates from the cabin. Brasstown Bald needs about 30 minutes; everything at Vogel and Neels Gap is close.

    House rules

    The owners mean it. Noise complaints can get the group evicted.
    11PM

    Quiet hours until 7 AM. A neighbor lives just across the creek and sound carries, so no loud music, talking or games outside.

    10:30PM

    Firepit cutoff. Douse it with water before anyone turns in.

    Bears

    They've been seen in the neighborhood. Bagged trash goes in the shed by the ramp, and the shed door stays shut.

    75°MAX

    Winter thermostat ceiling, five Nests in the cabin. Don't switch them off at checkout.

    Pond

    It's shared with the neighbors. No fishing, and nobody crosses the pond or the creek.

    Outside

    Smoke outside only. Ashtrays are provided. Porch string lights run on a timer by the swings, so don't leave them on overnight.

      Dawgs on Helenback: 7–2. Nine trips have landed on a Georgia game weekend, and the group watched every one. The losses were the 2011 SEC title game against LSU and Florida in 2020. Two trips fell on bye weeks.

      1. 2008I

        Helen · The Exec House

        Nov 21–23, 2008 · 10 people

        The original ten, in the Exec House over the weekend before Thanksgiving. The ledger's only clean note is ice skating. It was a Dawgs bye week, so the whole weekend was the cabin. One photo survives from year one: a card game spread across the dining table, red cups and a fire going, with a golf bag in the corner wearing a Christmas stocking.

        DawgsBye week
      2. 2009II

        Helen · Double Bogie

        Nov 22–25, 2009 · 10 people

        Peyton's invite went out under the subject "To Hell and Back," and the trip has had that name ever since. The house sat inside the gated Innsbruck golf community, keys from the office and a pass at the guard shack. It cost $50–75 a head, and the leftover money went to a keg. His note to anyone who didn't RSVP: you are no longer considered my friend. The photo that survives: the Double Bogie hot tub after dark, a koozie in one hand and a bottle in the next.

      3. 2010III

        Helen · Double Bogie again

        Nov 19–21, 2010 · 9 people

        Back to Double Bogie, and the year the ledger simply calls Midnight Golf. The proof survives: one full swing in pitch dark, and a daylight shot of the fairway running down the valley the next morning. Casey and Nathan also played a real round early Friday, so the Nut Cup has a sixteen-year-old ancestor. Cost was $55 a head, after Casey's reminder that all of you suck at replying to emails.

      4. 2011IV

        Helen · Eagle Eye Lodge

        Dec 2–4, 2011 · 14 people

        The first year at Eagle Eye. Most of what happened stays in the ledger, but one photo got out: the guys lined up on the stone overlook under a flat December sky, the ridgeline going grey behind them. Back inside, the Dawgs lost the SEC title to LSU.

        DawgsSEC Championship vs. LSU · L 10–42
      5. 2012V

        Blue Ridge · Rocky Top Lodge

        Dec 29, 2012 – Jan 1, 2013 · 29 people · all-time record

        New Year's, and the first trip outside Helen. Nathan's email said the cabin slept "19." Twenty-nine came, and the photos show how that worked: sleeping bags on every inch of floor. There was a poker table, a pool table and a packed hot tub, and on the 30th a walk out to a half-drained pond. At midnight the whole crowd jammed under the antlers with cups up while the ball dropped on TV, and then a bonfire in the pasture. Three kegs, no firearms for Dustin, no Chairman Meow, and something the ledger only calls Slap the Bag. Still the attendance record.

        DawgsCapital One Bowl vs. Nebraska · W 45–31
      6. 2013VI

        Blue Ridge · Erin Gray House

        Dec 27–29, 2013 · 11 people

        Erin Gray's family lent their brand-new lakehouse, so it was nearly free. It had no shower curtains yet, and the instructions were to think like you're camping, with wifi and heat. Eleven people came, and the crew piled onto the couch by the wreath-hung fireplace. On Sunday they hiked to a waterfall seen from a gorge overlook. When Connor worried the tradition was fading, Peyton wrote back: As long as the core cares about it, we will keep the tradition going.

        DawgsNo game. The Gator Bowl came Jan 1
      7. 2016VII

        Helen · Eagle Eye, again

        Dec 30, 2016 – Jan 2, 2017 · 17 people

        After two years off, the tradition came back to Eagle Eye, by then listed as Mountain Top Manor. Peyton brought the keg and the beer rifle. The photos show beer pong on red tables and a stone-walled hot tub, and the ledger remembers Kevin's raspberry champagne. The packing list said to bring athletic clothes (in case we hike — yeah right). There was a bowl-schedule tab even then, with the Dawgs beating TCU in the Liberty Bowl. Eleven purchases split thirteen ways came to $248 a head.

      8. 2020VIII

        Tuckasegee, NC · Sanctuary on Wolf Lake

        Nov 5–8, 2020 · 17 people

        The only Helenback outside Georgia, and the move to the first weekend of November, where it has stayed. It was election week, and the vote count ran on the TV all weekend. Satellite internet capped at 1.75 GB a day meant no streaming anyway. Wolf Lake made up for it with pink sunsets through the pines, long afternoons on the deck, ping pong downstairs and two waterfall hikes. Grace and Nathan cooked every meal, from lasagna to carnitas tacos. The next generation made its first appearance in baby carriers.

      9. 2021IX

        Morganton · 5 Star Lodge

        Nov 4–7, 2021 · 18 people

        The lodge was a working horse property at the end of a road where, the packet said in caps, DO NOT TRY AND USE YOUR GPS. It had a fenced pasture, a tennis court, a gazebo, two stories of windows facing the mountains and a red-felt pool table. The hot tub was full every night. C.P. (Chris Jones) noted that late checkout at $100 an hour could make a hungover Sunday expensive. Sarah Brown brought s'mores, and the big-group lunch at Fightingtown Tavern worked so well it went into the next year's packet.

      10. 2022X

        Morganton · Après Ski

        Nov 3–6, 2022 · 22 people

        Après Ski sat on Lake Blue Ridge. There was a pool and fire pit above the dock, a gym nobody needed, a pool table, and a theater room hung with tinsel for a party. Mornings were coffee on the flag-draped porch with babies on laps, and evenings were the fire pit over the water. The listing warned about hikers, deer and Sasquatch? on the gravel road. Nathan made smoked chicken pot pie, Curry made beef tenderloin, and Kellianne and Miguel made the game-day chili. Peyton's line on settling up: Some of that stuff you can't put a number on.
